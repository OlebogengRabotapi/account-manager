Problem Statement

The system manages bank accounts for users. It processes deposits, 
withdrawals, and transfers between accounts, while automatically 
detecting and blocking suspicious or fraudulent activity.

Rules

1. Insufficient Funds
   A withdrawal or transfer cannot exceed the account's current balance.

2. Rapid Transactions
   If an account has 3 or more withdrawal or transfer attempts within
   the last 10 seconds, the new attempt is blocked. This applies to
   BOTH withdrawals and transfers — a transfer is not a loophole
   around the withdrawal rate limit.

3. Unusual Spending Spike
   The system tracks each account's average outgoing transaction size,
   based on its successful withdrawals and transfers. If a new
   withdrawal or transfer is more than 5 times that average, it is
   blocked. Accounts with no prior successful outgoing transactions
   are exempt, since there is no average to compare against yet.

Requirements

1. Account Creation
   The system must be able to create a new account with an owner name
   and an opening balance. The opening balance cannot be negative.
   Each account is given a unique account number and starts with an
   empty transaction history.

2. Deposit
   A deposit must be greater than 0. On success, it is added to the
   account balance. Deposits are not subject to any of the fraud
   rules above.

3. Withdrawal
   A withdrawal must be greater than 0 and must pass all three rules
   above before the balance is changed. On success, the amount is
   subtracted from the balance and the account's outgoing transaction
   stats are updated.

4. Transfer
   A transfer requires both the sender and recipient accounts to
   exist and to be different accounts. The transfer amount must be
   greater than 0 and must pass all three rules above (applied to the
   sender) before either balance changes. On success, the amount is
   subtracted from the sender's balance and added to the recipient's
   balance in the same operation — if the credit side cannot complete,
   the debit must not happen either.

5. Rule Checking (shared logic)
   The insufficient-funds check, rapid-transaction check, and
   spending-spike check must be implemented once and reused by both
   Withdrawal and Transfer, rather than duplicated in each. This
   guarantees both operations are always checked against the same
   rules, in the same order, with no drift between them.

6. Recording Transactions
   Every withdrawal or transfer attempt — whether it passes or is
   blocked — should be recorded, so the system has an accurate record
   for rate-limiting and for later review. Only successful outgoing
   transactions count toward the running average used in Rule 3.

7. Account Data Tracked
   Each account stores: account number, owner name, balance, a short
   history of recent transaction attempts (for the rate-limit check),
   total number of successful outgoing transactions, and total amount
   of successful outgoing transactions (average is calculated from
   these two numbers, not stored separately).
